# Project ideas

Exploratory ideas are separate from committed release scope.

## Infrared operation and a bundled input kit

Proposed by Nicolás on2026-10-19. Consider a future WiggleControl version designed
for an infrared camera, allowing comfortable operation in darkness. Explore
whether an evolved version of the software could be delivered as a product kit
with a suitable camera and optional passive recognition aids: gloves, rings or
other wearables that make hands and gestures easier to identify reliably.

The aspiration is exceptionally robust recognition. Infrared compatibility and
the benefit of passive wearables are untested; infallibility is not an
established capability. This records the idea only: no hardware selection,
research, implementation or addition to the current Linux-preview milestone.
